# Work samples

Five short work samples by Bopanna N M, each a PDF in this repository. Each states its method, assumptions and limits.

| # | Sample | What it answers |
|---|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | [Quick commerce market sizing: Mysuru and Mangaluru](01_QuickCommerce_MarketSizing_Mysuru_Mangaluru.pdf) | About ₹150 crore a year (range ₹120 to 180 crore), three methods that overlap |
| 2 | [Subscriptions survey design (22 to 30 year olds)](02_Subscriptions_Survey_Design.pdf) | Questionnaire, sampling and analysis plan. Results added after collection |
| 3 | [India HR tech market sizing](03_India_HRTech_MarketSizing.pdf) | About ₹2,808 crore base case (range ₹1,092 to 5,616 crore), checked against vendors and analyst reports |
| 4 | [HR platform teardown: Darwinbox, Keka, Zoho People](04_HR_Platform_Teardown_Darwinbox_Keka_Zoho.pdf) | Which platform fits which company size, with vendor claims kept apart |
| 5 | [Job-switch survey design (22 to 30 year olds)](05_JobSwitch_Survey_Design.pdf) | Questionnaire, sampling and analysis plan. Results added after collection |

## How to read these
- Market sizes are desk estimates from public data. Assumptions are numbered and tested in a sensitivity table.
- The teardown relies on public pages and third-party summaries. Sources are graded.
- Survey samples are small and convenience-based. They describe respondents only.
